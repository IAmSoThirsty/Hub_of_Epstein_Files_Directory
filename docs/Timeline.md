# Timeline of Events

A chronological overview of key events related to the Epstein case.

## 1990s - Early Period

### Key Events
- Jeffrey Epstein establishes financial career
- Connections with various institutions and individuals develop
- Acquisition of properties including Palm Beach residence

## 2000s - Investigation Begins

### 2005
- **March:** Palm Beach Police Department begins investigation
- Initial complaints received

### 2006
- Investigation expands
- Evidence collected

### 2007
- Federal investigation begins
- Grand jury proceedings

### 2008
- **June:** Plea agreement reached in Florida
- Non-prosecution agreement executed
- Conviction on state charges
- Registration as sex offender

### 2009-2018
- Various civil lawsuits filed
- Continued legal challenges to 2008 agreement
- Investigative journalism continues

## 2010s - Renewed Investigation

### 2015
- Court documents from civil cases begin to be unsealed
- Media coverage intensifies

### 2018
- Miami Herald publishes extensive investigation
- Calls for re-examination of 2008 plea deal

### 2019

#### July
- **July 6:** Arrested at Teterboro Airport, New Jersey
- **July 8:** Charged in Southern District of New York
- **July 15:** Bail denied

#### August
- **August 10:** Found unresponsive in cell at Metropolitan Correctional Center
- **August 10:** Death declared
- **August 10-11:** Immediate investigations launched

#### Post-August
- Medical examiner's report
- FBI investigation
- DOJ Inspector General review

### 2020

#### January-June
- Document releases continue
- Civil cases proceed
- Investigation into associates continues

#### July
- **July 2:** Ghislaine Maxwell arrested
- Federal charges announced

#### Rest of Year
- Pretrial proceedings
- Additional document releases

## 2021

### Trial & Conviction
- **November-December:** Ghislaine Maxwell trial
- **December 29:** Guilty verdict on multiple counts

### Ongoing
- Civil cases continue
- Document unsealing continues
- Additional investigations

## 2022

### Sentencing & Appeals
- **June 28:** Ghislaine Maxwell sentenced
- Appeals process begins
- Civil settlements announced

### Document Releases
- Continued unsealing of court documents
- Media investigations publish findings

## 2023

### Legal Proceedings
- Appeals continue
- Additional civil cases filed
- Settlement negotiations

### Document Access
- More documents made public
- Database compilations created

## 2024

### Current Status
- Ongoing civil litigation
- Continued document releases
- Public records requests processed
- Research and investigation continues
